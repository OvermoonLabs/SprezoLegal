Sprezo is a behavioral skills training mobile application that helps users practice real-world conversations with AI personas to build social and dating confidence.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use Sprezo, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your data. It applies to all users of the App globally, with specific provisions for users in India (under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), the European Union (under GDPR), and California, USA (under CCPA/CPRA).

By creating an account or using Sprezo, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. What information we collect

### 2.1 Account information (when you sign in)
- Name, email address, and profile picture — provided by Google when you choose "Sign in with Google"
- A unique user identifier generated by us
- Account creation timestamp

### 2.2 Practice & usage data
- Conversation transcripts you exchange with AI personas (stored so we can provide feedback and track progression)
- Onboarding quiz answers (intent, conflict response, vibe, current state, pacing)
- Session metadata: persona selected, scores, completion status, timestamps, recommendations shown
- Gamification data: XP, level, current streak, sessions used per month / per day
- Feedback ratings you give and AI-generated feedback you receive

### 2.3 Subscription & purchase data
- Subscription tier (trial / Spark+ / Pro) and renewal status
- Subscription start, renewal, cancellation, and refund events
- Practice Pack purchases and Friend Invite redemption history
- **We do NOT store credit card, debit card, UPI, or bank account details.** All payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing (and Apple App Store in the future). Synqua Games never sees your payment instrument.

### 2.4 Device & analytics data
- App version, OS type and version, device model (anonymized)
- In-app event analytics (screen views, feature usage, conversion funnel)
- Crash logs and performance diagnostics
- Approximate region inferred from IP address (not precise location)

### 2.5 What we don't collect
- We do **not** access your precise GPS location, contacts, microphone, camera, photos, files, calendar, SMS, or call logs.
- We do **not** collect government-issued identifiers (PAN, Aadhaar, passport).
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your data only for the following purposes:

- **Service delivery:** authenticating you, saving your progress, generating AI feedback, managing your subscription, processing your purchases.
- **Personalization:** using your quiz answers and session history to recommend personas that fit your skill level and growth goals.
- **Product improvement:** anonymized analytics to find drop-off points, measure persona effectiveness, A/B test pricing and onboarding flows.
- **Transactional communication:** receipts, account changes, security notices. (We do not send marketing emails without your explicit opt-in.)
- **Abuse prevention:** rate limiting, fraud detection on Friend Invite redemptions and Practice Pack purchases.
- **Legal compliance:** meeting our obligations under Indian law and other applicable jurisdictions.

We do **not** sell your personal data to anyone.

## 4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR users)

Where GDPR applies (EU/UK residents), we process your data on these legal bases:
- **Consent** — for analytics and personalization (you can withdraw any time)
- **Contract** — to provide the App and manage your subscription
- **Legitimate interest** — for fraud prevention and product improvement, balanced against your rights
- **Legal obligation** — when required by Indian or EU law

## 5. Who we share your information with

We share data only with these processors, each under formal data-processing agreements:

| Processor | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Google LLC** | Authentication (Google Sign-In) and Play Store billing | Email, name, profile picture, purchase events |
| **MongoDB Atlas** | Database hosting | All app data (encrypted at rest) |
| **Anthropic PBC** | AI generation (Claude Sonnet 4) — generates persona replies and feedback | Conversation transcripts for inference only |
| **RevenueCat Inc.** _(once integrated)_ | Subscription receipt validation and webhook handling | Purchase events, user_id, subscription tier |
| **Cloud infrastructure providers** | Hosting | Encrypted backend traffic |

**Important AI disclosure:** When you chat with a persona, your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4 model. Anthropic processes the data only to generate the response; **Anthropic does not use your conversation content to train any AI model** (per their commercial API terms).

We do not share personal data with advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing platforms.

If we are legally required to disclose your data (court order, government request under Indian IT Act 2000, etc.), we will do so only when strictly required by law and will notify you when permitted.

## 6. AI and your conversations

When you chat with a Sprezo persona:
- Your messages are sent securely to Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4 model to generate the persona's reply and your session feedback.
- We retain transcripts on our servers so you can review past sessions and so the recommendation engine can suggest your next persona.
- We do **not** use your conversation content to train any AI model.
- You can delete any individual session from Settings → "My Sessions" → swipe to delete _(coming soon)_.
- You can delete your entire account and all conversation data from Settings → "Delete Account".

## 7. Your rights

You have the following rights regardless of where you live:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correction** — fix anything inaccurate
- **Deletion / Right to be forgotten** — delete your account and all associated data
- **Portability / Export** — download your data as CSV (Settings → "Export my data")
- **Object** — opt out of specific processing (e.g., analytics)
- **Withdraw consent** — at any time, by signing out, deleting your account, or uninstalling the App

### India-specific rights (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)
As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you also have the right to:
- Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity
- Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India
- Receive a clear summary of your data when requested

### EU/UK-specific rights (GDPR)
You also have the right to:
- Object to automated decision-making
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

### California-specific rights (CCPA/CPRA)
You have the right to know what categories of data we collect, opt out of any "sale" or "share" (we don't sell or share data for advertising — this is automatic), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

**To exercise any of these rights**, email **contactus@synqua.com** from the email address linked to your Sprezo account. We respond within 30 days (15 days for India DPDP requests where feasible).

## 9. Children's privacy

Sprezo is intended for users aged **18 and older only**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we discover an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it immediately and notify a verified guardian where possible.

Under India's DPDP Act, processing of a child's (under 18) personal data requires verifiable parental consent — we choose simply not to serve under-18 users.

## 10. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures:
- HTTPS (TLS) encryption for all data in transit
- Encrypted MongoDB Atlas database connections
- Scoped access tokens and OAuth 2.0 authentication
- Admin endpoints locked to specific authorized email addresses
- Routine security patches and dependency updates
- Rate limiting on sensitive endpoints

No system is 100% secure. **If we ever experience a data breach that puts your personal data at risk**, we will notify affected users within **72 hours** by email and in-app, and report the incident to the Data Protection Board of India and any other relevant authorities as required by law.

## 11. International data transfers

Your data is processed and stored on cloud infrastructure that may be located outside India (currently in the United States, on Google Cloud / MongoDB Atlas / Anthropic infrastructure). Where personal data is transferred internationally, we ensure equivalent protection through:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU/UK users
- Synqua Games' internal data-protection commitments for India users
- Compliance with the destination country's data protection laws

By using Sprezo, you consent to your data being processed in those jurisdictions.
